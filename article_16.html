The Fading Elderly in Silence

My name is Jiahao, a student from a junior high school in my community. I'd like to introduce an old man called Bei, I don't know his real name, in our community.

Shining in the sky, the sun set his light on patches of sakuras and a stone table, bringing a sense of comfort. "According to fork history, Dong Zuo and Lv Bu are a famous couple.." My friends and I were sitting around the stone table to listen to Bei's story. Bei is a wise and interesting man. He knew many things but was different from others——he seldom used memes, even popular ones.

I still remembered it was a cloudy day. My friends and I were sitting surrounding Bei as usual. One of my friends asked him a question: "Mr.Bei, why I never heard of your lover?". Everything became silence. Mr.Bei, usually with smile on his face, had some tears filled in his eyes.

After a while, Bei finally said "Ahh…It's because I haven't loved anyone, even the most beautiful girl I've met."

Two years later. My classmates and I went to junior high schools. We were busy studying and didn't meet Bei even once.

Occasionally, according to my Chinese homework, I went out my home to find "the beauty of Autumn" and passed Bei's house. I heard a low voice from the house. I got closer to Bei's house, looking through his windows. Bei was in the bed and repeated something. It sounded like a name, a girl's name. "Yuer.."

Then I never see Bei.

A few days later, I was told by my father that Bei was dead. At first, no one noticed his disappearance. It was supposed that he had dead 3 days before my uncle found him dead. The full moon was shining in the sky. Leaves fell in front of a door.

There was a letter lying on his wooden table, written some words: "I love my dear, Yuban, forever. I made it."
